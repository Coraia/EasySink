# Picturebook illustrations

## Sentence storyboard v2 · 2026-10-19

`chapter-01-story-v2.webp` through `chapter-06-story-v2.webp` are six actual generated 2×2 storyboard atlases. In each atlas, top-left, top-right and bottom-left represent three distinct sentence events; bottom-right is an unused establishing cover. No cropping or camera motion is used to invent different events. `story-manifest.json` maps the eighteen exact source sentences to their frames. The application switches frames on hover, click and keyboard navigation.

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool using `little-prince-atlas.webp` as the character/style reference. All six final prompts, scene directions and output provenance are in `story-generation.json`. The original tool PNG files are preserved in the Codex generated-images directory; project-bound delivery uses these local WebP assets.

The atlases were visually checked for different actions and consistent characters: stargazing / asking questions / telescope; blooming / watering / protecting the rose; leaving / scarf in the wind / homesickness; lone pilot / meeting / walking together; lone fox / cautious distance / close friendship; stars / waiting rose / connected friends.

## Original atmosphere atlas v1

Asset: `little-prince-atlas.webp` (1536 × 1024, 3 columns × 2 rows).

Generated using the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-19. Converted to WebP for local delivery. CSS selects each cell; no external image request is needed. Beautiful delicate gouache and watercolor on softly textured paper, poetic, sophisticated children's book, cinematic compositions, muted ochre, sage, pale cream with midnight blue, clear focal subject, generous sky, handpainted stars. No text, no typography, no panel numbers, no lettering or watermarks. Panel top left: tiny prince on a small round asteroid with three little volcanoes looking across deep blue starry sky, warm golden planet. Panel top middle: prince tenderly kneeling beside one red rose under a clear glass dome in a warm sage and cream garden on tiny planet. Panel top right: prince carried through a dreamy dusk sky by a flock of migrating birds with golden scarf streaming, tiny planets in the distance. Panel bottom left: prince meeting a stranded aviator by a small vintage propeller airplane in wide golden Sahara dunes, dawn sky. Panel bottom middle: prince sitting quietly near a friendly orange fox under a leafy tree in golden meadow, gentle sunset. Panel bottom right: prince sitting on warm crescent of a tiny planet looking at luminous stars, rich midnight blue and golden scarf, peaceful thoughtful ending. The grid boundaries must be exactly at 1/3, 2/3 width and 1/2 height so each panel can be shown individually as a CSS background. Make the six images distinct and detailed, finished editorial art.
