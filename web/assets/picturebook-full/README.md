# The Little Prince — full English key-event picture-book edition

27 chapters, 16,485 whitespace-delimited English words, 60 key-event illustrations. This is the complete story text of the edition below, including its source spelling, not an expanded version of the 18-sentence demonstration. The original dedication is available in the reader's edition details.

Author: Antoine de Saint-Exupéry. English translation: Iraklis Lampadariou. Publisher: Saita Publications, July 2015. ISBN 978-618-5147-45-7. Source edition license: **Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 3.0 Unported (CC BY-NC 3.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/.

Edition listing: https://www.openrightslibrary.com/the-little-prince-ebook/
Retrieved public text mirror: https://online.flipbuilder.com/npkog/rsmg/ (85 HTML pages, printed pagination through 88; story in HTML pages 5–81).
The publisher's historical PDF link was unavailable. Text was obtained from the public HTML text layer, not from an OCR reconstruction or an alternate English translation.

Changes: rejoined layout whitespace, wrapped lines and separated initial drop caps. Removed printed page/chapter numerals from body paragraphs. Source wording and spelling retained. Added Chinese navigation titles and new AI-generated key-event illustrations; original illustration plates are not reproduced.

`book.json` contains the complete chapter text, paragraph arrays, SHA-256 values, source attribution, and key-event anchors. A sentence belongs to the most recent preceding event anchor. Several sentences may deliberately share a picture.

`key-events-01.webp` through `key-events-15.webp` are 15 newly generated 2×2 atlases. All four equal cells are used; cell 0 top-left, 1 top-right, 2 bottom-left, 3 bottom-right. Character/style reference: `../picturebook/chapter-04-story-v2.webp`. Two superseded originals (07 and 13) are retained; the active edition uses `07-v2` and `13-v2` to distinguish the tippler from the pilot and remove a non-source village behind the well. Full exact prompts, reference, original PNG basenames and revisions are in `generation.json`. Generated with built-in image_gen, not the configurable external image API. The original PNGs remain in the Codex generated_images directory; deployed WebP copies live here.

Reproduction and verification material is in `outputs/picturebook-full-20261019/`: archived HTML/text pages and hashes, `extract_source.py`, `plan_illustrations.py`, source identity checks, complete generation prompts and original paths, real browser observations and screenshots. `source-copyright.txt` retains the source edition's copyright/license page.

The original 6-scene/18-sentence retelling and its art remain separate in `../picturebook/`.
